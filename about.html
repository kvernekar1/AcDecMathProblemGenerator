About the Creator

Hi guys! I'm Kedar V. I am currently a senior in high school and I created this website to help students like you prepare for the Academic Decathlon math section!

Throughout the past 3 years of me participating in Academic Decathlon, I noticed that this is the subject that most people tend to struggle with. In all the Academic Decathlon competitions I've been a part of, math has consistently seen some of the lowest scores.

Even in my school, where the Academic Decathlon class is filled with high-achieving students, math remains a challenging subject for them.

That is the reason I have created this website - to provide resources, practice problems, and guidance to help students improve their math skills and succeed in the competition.

What makes me qualified to make this website?

As mentioned before, I have been actively participating in Academic Decathlon for the past three years, and I have gained valuable insights into the challenges students face in the math section.

Additionally, here are some of my achievements that demonstrate my proficiency in math:

  • Scored 900+ on the Academic Decathlon Math section at nationals, earning me 4th place.
  • Qualified for the American Invitational Mathematics Examination (AIME) four times in a row.
  • Placed 8th in the UIL Number Sense State Competition out of the 830 UIL District competitors (top 0.96% in the state).

All these accomplishments I have listed require a need to do math problems both quickly and accurately, and I hope to convey what I have learned through gaining accomplishments like those listed above.

What is the goal of this website?

From my experiences, I found out that the goal to getting such a high score (aside from learning the content thoroughly) is to practice consistently.

Practicing these problems consistently is the only way to gain the intuition needed to solve them quickly and accurately, which is the name of the game for Academic Decathlon Math.

Therefore, the goal of this website is to provide you with a platform to practice these problems consistently and effectively to help you gain the intuition you need to do well.

This website provides you with virtually unlimited amounts of practice problems (which are AI-generated), as well as the ability to practice by topic and to test yourself using the test feature.
