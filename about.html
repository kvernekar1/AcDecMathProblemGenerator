About the Creator

About page sections

Hi guys! I'm Kedar. I am currently a senior in high school and I created this website to help students like you prepare for the Academic Decathlon

Throughout the past 3 years of me participating in Academic Decathlon, I noticed that there aren't a lot of resources to come by, at least in the AcDec teams around me.

Often, my team and other teams I've seen only have access to meager amounts of practice tests, and I felt like teams can do so much more if they had access to more problems.

That is the reason I have created this website: to provide resources and practice problems to help students prepare for the seven objective tests.

Additionally, I have made a guide for the math section that goes over several important topics. This guide should only be use as a supplement and not a replacement for the math guide.

With that being said, I hope you find this website useful!
